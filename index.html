1,000 simulated market paths: monthly returns are drawn at random with the volatility (σ) you set, calibrated so the median path compounds at exactly your growth rates — the deterministic answer above is the middle path, not a promise. “Success” means the pot funds the spending for the full retirement (and keeps any end cushion). Random draws have no memory and no fat tails, so treat the odds as a stress lens, not a guarantee.
