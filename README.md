# RetirePlan — phased-contribution retirement calculator

A 100% client-side web app with no build step, network calls, or accounts — its only dependency, D3.js, is vendored locally as `d3.min.js` so `file://` and offline use work — that answers "How much can I spend per month in retirement, in today's dollars?"; open `index.html` in any browser or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server 8765`, and run the dependency-free financial-core tests with `node --test test/core.test.js`. Unlike most calculators, its contribution period and retirement date are separate clocks, so you can start later, stop early, and coast before and after contributing, with optional contribution escalation, other (inflation-indexed) income with a delayed-start bridge, an end cushion, and a target-spending check whose gap solver and back-fill button (in both directions, with a lock that keeps a funded target funded) write the needed extra monthly contribution or lump sum back into the plan.

The app models Saving (current savings plus monthly contributions), Coasting (no contributions, compounding only), and Spending (level, inflation-indexed withdrawals solved to end at ~$0 after the chosen retirement length) as a monthly simulation, and reports headline sustainable spending in today's dollars alongside sensitivity to ±2%/yr in average returns, the pot at retirement and what contributions vs. growth built it, an animated D3 balance chart with phase bands, and a Monte Carlo card of 1,000 fixed-seed paths (lognormal returns calibrated so the deterministic answer is the median path) giving the chance the tested spending lasts, the spending that lasts in the adjustable Monte Carlo success target (85% by default), and a histogram, plus optional down-year-cut and risk-based-guardrails stress-test strategies that cut or re-solve spending only inside that Monte Carlo comparison; defaults ($1M now, $0/mo, 7% growth before and 4.5% in retirement, 2.5% inflation, retire in 10 years) yield about $5,423/mo in today's dollars, with inputs clamped to sane ranges, persisted only in browser `localStorage`, and shareable via a compact URL-hash link that never overwrites a saved plan until edited.

Method, in brief: contributions and withdrawals occur at the start of each month before growth, with annual rates converted to effective monthly rates and sustainable spending solved as a level real annuity-due at the real retirement return, everything restated in today's dollars unless labelled "future $"; the code lives in `index.html` (markup/form), `js/core.js` (pure financial core), `js/ui.js`, `js/charts.js`, `js/render.js`, and `js/main.js` (persistence and wiring, no build step, printing shows results only, reduced-motion and ARIA text summaries supported), and its limits are constant-return/inflation headline assumptions, independently drawn lognormal Monte Carlo returns with inflation fixed (no fat tails, clustering, taxes, or fees), so its odds are a stress lens and a planning illustration — not financial advice.
